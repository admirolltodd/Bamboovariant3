Our story

Local since 2007.

What started as a small Crestview restaurant grew into three Bamboo locations across Florida's Emerald Coast. The footprint changed; the family-owned identity stayed at the center.

Sushi bar and hibachi kitchen.

Bamboo brings sushi, hibachi, noodles, curry, appetizers and other Japanese-inspired favorites together under one roof so mixed groups do not have to choose one kind of meal.

Feeding more than a table?

For sushi trays, hibachi platters or larger orders, call your preferred location so the team can confirm current options and timing.
